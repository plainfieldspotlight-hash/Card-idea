❓ New here? How to read this dashboard (30 seconds)

What this app does: it tracks card prices every day, learns from how they've moved before, and estimates where each card's price is heading over the next 7 days.

  • Predicted move — the model's best guess, e.g. +5% means it expects the price to be about 5% higher in a week. The small range next to it ("−1% … +12%") is the realistic spread of outcomes — wide range = less certain.
  • Chance ↑ — the probability the price ends higher in 7 days. 70%+ means the model is fairly confident; ~50% means it's a coin flip.
  • 💰 Money makers only — collectors know commons/uncommons sell as bulk, so the dashboard focuses on the chase rarities that actually trade by default: Full Arts, Illustration Rares (IR/SIR), Ultra/Secret/Hyper Rares, and Holos. The tier chips above the card table jump straight to one tier; flip the 💰 button off if you ever want bulk back.
  • High-confidence buys — cards where the model is confident and even its cautious estimate is positive, grouped by price bracket.
  • Deal radar — those same promising cards, with links to buy them on eBay.
  • Watchlist & alerts — star ☆ any card and you'll get an alert when the model turns bullish on it or its price swings hard.
  • My collection — log cards you own to see your total value, profit, and what the model expects your collection to do this week.
  • Model report card — the honesty section: every past prediction is graded against what the price actually did. If the model is wrong, this is where it shows.

Predictions are estimates from public price data — a reason to look closer, never a guarantee. Not financial advice.
